OUR SERVICES

Comprehensive Support for Hotel Pre-Opening Requirements

Launching a hotel involves numerous interconnected responsibilities. Every department must be prepared, every key position must be considered, and the people involved must understand their responsibilities.

SK Management provides staffing solutions focused on the management and operational requirements of hotels preparing to open.

1. Hotel Pre-Opening Staffing Solutions

The pre-opening phase is one of the most important stages in a hotel's development. Establishing an appropriate workforce early can help hospitality businesses organise responsibilities, prepare departments, and establish a service-oriented working culture.

We work to understand the staffing requirements of each property and support the identification of suitable personnel for the roles required.

Our approach can support:

- Workforce requirement planning.
- Identification of key management and operational positions.
- Staffing coordination according to project requirements.
- Department-wise personnel requirements.
- Support for building teams ahead of the opening date.
- Coordination around operational readiness priorities.

The objective is to help hospitality businesses approach their opening with a more organised staffing structure.

2. Hotel Management Team Staffing

Strong leadership is essential to establishing a hotel's operating standards, departmental coordination, and guest-service culture.

We support management staffing requirements based on the positions and experience levels needed by the property.

Depending on the hotel's structure and requirements, relevant positions may include:

- General management.
- Hotel operations management.
- Front office management.
- Housekeeping management.
- Food and beverage management.
- Human resources and personnel management.
- Sales and revenue-related management.
- Other departmental leadership positions.

The final staffing structure depends on the property's size, service offering, organisational requirements, and agreed scope of work.

Our focus is on helping clients identify the management talent appropriate to their hotel's operational objectives.

3. Hotel Operational Team Staffing

A hotel delivers its promise through the people who manage daily activities and interact with guests. Operational teams are central to maintaining service standards, supporting guest satisfaction, and ensuring that departments work together effectively.

We support operational staffing requirements according to the hotel's needs.

Potential staffing categories include:

Front Office and Guest Services

- Front office personnel.
- Reception and guest-service staff.
- Guest relations personnel.
- Reservation-related positions.

Housekeeping

- Housekeeping supervisors.
- Room attendants.
- Public-area housekeeping personnel.
- Other housekeeping positions required by the property.

Food and Beverage

- Restaurant service personnel.
- Food and beverage supervisors.
- Service associates.
- Other restaurant or hospitality service roles.

Kitchen Operations

- Chefs and kitchen personnel.
- Kitchen supervisors.
- Food preparation and kitchen support positions.

Additional Operational Departments

Other roles may be considered according to the property's facilities, operating model, and staffing requirements.

Specific roles, availability, experience requirements, and engagement terms can be discussed during the initial project consultation.

4. Department-Wise Workforce Planning

Every hotel requires a staffing structure that reflects its scale, positioning, facilities, and anticipated operating requirements.

An appropriate workforce plan considers departmental responsibilities, reporting relationships, operational priorities, and the anticipated needs of the property.

We work with clients to understand their staffing priorities and the types of management and operational personnel required for their planned opening.

Our focus is on supporting a structured approach to workforce requirements rather than treating every property as identical.

5. Support for New Hotel Launches

A new hotel launch requires coordination across multiple functions. Staffing requirements should be considered alongside opening schedules, departmental preparation, and the hotel's intended service standards.

SK Management can discuss staffing requirements for new hotel projects at different stages of preparation.

Whether a property is approaching its planned opening or is in the process of developing its departmental structure, our aim is to understand the project's needs and determine how our staffing services may support its requirements.

OUR PROCESS

A Structured Approach to Hotel Staffing

Every hotel project has its own timeline, operational priorities, and workforce requirements. Our approach begins with understanding those needs.

Step 1: Understand Your Project

We begin by learning about your hotel, its planned opening, operational structure, facilities, and staffing priorities.

Step 2: Identify Staffing Requirements

We discuss the management and operational positions required, along with relevant experience levels, departmental needs, and anticipated timelines.

Step 3: Discuss Suitable Personnel

Based on the agreed requirements and available candidates, we discuss potential staffing options and the next steps for the assignment.

Step 4: Coordinate the Staffing Process

We coordinate with the client regarding role requirements, selection arrangements, and other agreed staffing-related activities.

Step 5: Prepare for the Opening

Our objective is to support the hotel's workforce preparation according to the agreed requirements, timeline, and scope of engagement.

The exact process may vary depending on the property, positions involved, and terms agreed with the client.

What does SK Management specialise in?

SK Management focuses on providing skilled staff for hotel pre-opening requirements, including management and operational teams.

What is hotel pre-opening staffing?

Hotel pre-opening staffing involves identifying and preparing the workforce required before a hotel begins welcoming guests. Depending on the property, this may include management positions, departmental supervisors, and operational personnel.

When should a hotel begin planning its staffing requirements?

Staffing preparation should be considered early enough to align recruitment and workforce planning with the hotel's opening schedule. The appropriate timing depends on the size of the property, the number of departments, and the complexity of its operations.

Can staffing requirements be discussed for different hotel sizes?

Yes. Staffing requirements can be discussed according to the property's planned operating model, facilities, departmental structure, and personnel needs.
